# ADR-004 — Fit / Intent / Engagement Scoring

Status: Proposed
Owner: Timothy King
Notion: https://app.notion.com/p/493a3c3abad24c45948658d759bc44eb

Imported 9 Oct 2026 from Notion 02 (page last edited 29 Sep 2026), verbatim.
Proposed: editable, not in force.

## Decision

Maintain fit, intent, and engagement as separate, explainable scores. Use reason codes, freshness, decay, negative evidence, and outcome calibration before introducing a combined priority score.

## Guardrails

- Version every scoring policy
- Retain supporting evidence
- Avoid automatic high-impact stage changes initially
- Measure recommendations against outcomes
