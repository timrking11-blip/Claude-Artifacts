## Decision

Represent the customer journey as linked evidence across account, contact, signal, content, website event, outreach, meeting, proposal, opportunity, and revenue—not only as a linear funnel.

## Guardrails

- Preserve source and timestamps
- Record identity confidence
- Separate observation from interpretation
- Support correction and supersession
