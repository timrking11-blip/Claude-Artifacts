## Decision

Begin with first touch, lead source, latest touch, and influenced touchpoints. Defer algorithmic multi-touch attribution until identity coverage and conversion volume are sufficient.

## Guardrails

- Version attribution rules
- Separate sourced from influenced pipeline
- Preserve unattributed outcomes
- Never represent probabilistic identity as certain
