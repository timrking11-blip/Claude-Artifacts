## Why now: the market and the economy
Community banks are earning more: quarterly net income rose 8.2% and the net interest margin widened 10 basis points in Q2 2026 ([FDIC, Q2 2026](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-second-quarter-2026)). At the same time regulators are cutting fixed burden, with higher FDICIA thresholds and wider 18-month exam eligibility announced in 2026 ([FDIC, 2026](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/agencies-reduce-regulatory-burden-community-banks-increase-eligibility-18)). The revised Section 1071 rule narrows coverage and moves compliance to 1 January 2028 ([Consumer Finance Monitor, May 2026](https://www.consumerfinancemonitor.com/2026/05/22/cfpb-finalizes-revised-1071-rule/)). HMDA moves the other way: from the 2026 filing year every edit must be cleared before submission ([FFIEC FIG 2026](https://ffiec.cfpb.gov/documentation/fig/2026/overview)). The sector is mid-cycle with budget available but less regulatory urgency, so the buying case shifts from fear of examiners to hours saved and filings that pass the first time.

## The problem in front of Claridi.ai
1. The customer base is getting smaller: 3,818 community banks at Q2 2026, and the count keeps falling through consolidation ([FDIC, Q2 2026](https://www.fdic.gov/news/speeches/2026/fdic-quarterly-banking-profile-second-quarter-2026)) [supported]. Each lost bank is a lost seat, so share per bank matters more than logo count.
2. HMDA and fair lending are crowded: Ncontracts, RiskExec, RATA Associates, ComplianceTech and Marquis all sell into the same compliance officer ([Ncontracts](https://www.ncontracts.com/products/fair-lending-compliance-software), [RiskExec](https://riskexec.com/)) [supported]. A validator alone competes on price against incumbents already in the bank's vendor file.
3. Marketing-compliance review has fewer rivals, with Kadince the named one ([Kadince](https://kadince.com/solutions/institution/community-banks)) [needs stipulation]. It is also the least deadline-driven purchase in the bundle.
4. Deregulation removes the easiest argument, the new rule the bank must meet, outside HMDA filing ([FDIC, 2026](https://www.fdic.gov/news/press-releases/2026/agencies-reduce-regulatory-burden-community-banks-increase-eligibility-18)) [needs stipulation]. The value case has to be proved in examiner findings avoided and staff time returned.

The engagement prevents a launch that leads with the crowded product into a shrinking segment and spends the first sales year displacing incumbents.

## Approach
**Segment and demand map** (3 weeks). Closes which asset band and charter type to lead with, from public call report, HMDA reporter and exam-cycle data. Gate: Claridi.ai approves the target segment and the three buying triggers.

**Entry-product test** (3 weeks). Closes which module opens the door, using buyer interviews with compliance officers and a side-by-side against the named incumbents. Gate: Claridi.ai approves the lead offer and the proof points it must show.

**Go-to-market plan** (2 weeks). Closes the first-year plan: channel, filing-season timing and the displacement story. Gate: Claridi.ai signs off on the plan and the kill threshold.

## What we'd need to qualify this
1. What is the go-to-market decision in one sentence, and by when does it have to be made?
2. What happens if it slips past the next HMDA filing season?
3. What is already fixed: the price model, the segment, or a channel partner?
4. What is the hardest constraint: sales capacity, product readiness, or proof from live customers?
5. Who else has a say, and who signs off on bringing in an outside advisor?
