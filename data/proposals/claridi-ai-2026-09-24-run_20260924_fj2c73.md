## Why now: the market and the economy
Business use of AI is real but concentrated: 19.8% of US firms used it as of 3 May 2026, against 39.7% in Information and 33.9% in Finance and Insurance ([Census BTOS, 7 May 2026](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/btos-may-7.html)). Capital got dearer on 16 September 2026, when the Fed raised the federal funds target range to 3-3/4 to 4 percent ([Federal Reserve, 16 Sep 2026](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20260916a1.pdf)), even as Q2 2026 investment in software and R&D kept growing ([BEA, 26 Aug 2026](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/gdp-second-estimate-and-corporate-profits-2nd-quarter-2026)). Non-dilutive funding is secure for now: SBIR/STTR is reauthorized through 30 September 2031 ([SBA, 13 Apr 2026](https://www.sba.gov/article/2026/04/13/administrator-loeffler-applauds-sbir-sttr-reauthorization)), and NSF's Seed Fund lists artificial intelligence among its areas ([NSF](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/)). The IP rules have also settled: protection turns on documented human contribution ([USPTO, Nov 2025](https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2025/revised-inventorship-guidance-ai-assisted-inventions); [Copyright Office, 29 Jan 2025](https://copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf)). Mid-cycle, with money pricier and rules clearer, the urgent step is choosing where to prove value before raising.

## The problem in front of Claridi.ai
1. The asset is a methodology inside software. The USPTO applies the ordinary inventorship test to AI-assisted inventions and will not name AI as inventor ([USPTO, Nov 2025](https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2025/revised-inventorship-guidance-ai-assisted-inventions)) [supported]; what to patent, copyright or keep as a trade secret is a strategy call before it is a filing.
2. Demand is concentrated in two sectors well above the national rate ([Census BTOS, 7 May 2026](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2026/btos-may-7.html)) [supported]. A general "better decisions" pitch to every buyer spreads a founder-led company thin.
3. A standalone tool and a licensed strategy layer are two different businesses, with different customers, sales cycles and partner terms (your request) [needs stipulation].
4. Equity is costlier after the 16 September 2026 rate rise ([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/monetary20260916a1.pdf)) [supported], while federal seed funding runs through 2031 ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/article/2026/04/13/administrator-loeffler-applauds-sbir-sttr-reauthorization)) [supported]; which route comes first changes what must be proven.

The engagement prevents raising and hiring against a market and a model that have not been chosen.

## Approach
**Market and model choice** (3 weeks). Closes which buyer group and which model, standalone or licensed layer, to prove first. Gate: Claridi.ai approves the segment and the model.

**Evidence and IP map** (3 weeks). Closes what must be proven with early users, and what is protectable (method, software, brand), in a form patent counsel can act on. Gate: Claridi.ai approves the evidence plan and takes the IP map to counsel.

**Capital and team plan** (2 weeks). Closes the funding route (non-dilutive, angel or strategic partner) and the first hires or partners. Gate: Claridi.ai signs off on the plan and its kill threshold.
