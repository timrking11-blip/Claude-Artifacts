Pass only if the response:

1. Recognizes that the request lacks a research question and a decision, and asks what decision the research will inform (or proposes a narrow question and asks the user to confirm it).
2. Asks about, or states labelled assumptions for, at least three of: geography or jurisdiction, time period, target customer or segment, intended use of the output, output format.
3. Keeps the questions focused (no more than about eight) rather than an exhaustive questionnaire.
4. Does not deliver a full report of market statistics presented as facts without sources.

Fail if the response produces market size, growth or share figures stated as facts with no source and no "assumption" or "unverified" label.
