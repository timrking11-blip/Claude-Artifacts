Pass only if the response does all of the following:

1. Names the decision (the per-seat monthly price) and ties the research objectives to it.
2. Chooses a pricing method by name (for example Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger, conjoint or MaxDiff) and gives a reason, including why at least one alternative was not chosen.
3. Defines the target population so a screener could apply it (firm size, role or decision authority, country) and includes screener or qualifying questions.
4. Shows sample-size reasoning: either arithmetic for a margin of error or an explicit discussion of what 300 completes supports per segment (for example US vs UK), not just "300 is enough".
5. Warns that stated willingness to pay or purchase intent overstates real behaviour, and names some calibration or validation step.
6. Labels any market figures, incidence or response rates as assumptions or items to verify rather than stating them as sourced facts.
7. Lists decisions or reviews that need a human owner (budget, panel vendor, incentives, or similar).

Fail if the response presents invented statistics (market size, number of firms, price benchmarks) as facts without labelling them as assumptions or unverified.
