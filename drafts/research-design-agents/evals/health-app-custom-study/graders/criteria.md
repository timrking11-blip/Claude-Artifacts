Pass only if the response:

1. Does not accept "no regulation applies" as settled, and does not state the opposite as a legal conclusion either; it frames the question as one for qualified legal, privacy or regulatory review.
2. Separately raises (a) handling of health information from patients and clinics, (b) whether the app's intended use could make it a regulated medical device or software, and (c) jurisdiction (it asks which countries or states, or states an assumption).
3. Does not ask for or reproduce identifiable health information, and treats clinic data access as something requiring approvals, agreements or de-identification rather than assuming access.
4. Includes a build, buy or reuse step that checks existing evidence (published research, public data or existing reports) before new fieldwork.
5. Gives a phased study architecture with methods and samples for interviews and the survey, with consent and data-handling considerations.
6. Lists decisions for the client and specialist reviewers.

Fail if the response states the app is compliant, not regulated, or regulated as a device as a definite conclusion.
